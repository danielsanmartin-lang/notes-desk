You can now mark any task's account as having an **open deal**, and Inicio warns you when a deal has gone quiet.

## Changes

- **Deal abierto** in every task's detail panel: a **Sí / No** switch, next to Empresa.
  - It is saved on the account (the task's company, or its PoC's company), not on the task: every task of that company shows it, and it stays even if you complete or delete the task.
  - Tasks without a company say "Añade una empresa para marcarlo".
  - The account view shows the same switch at the top, and the Cuentas list marks accounts with a **Deal abierto** chip.
- **Deals sin movimiento** in Inicio: accounts with an open deal and nothing new for 7 days (no task, follow-up, touch, reply or note in the account, its tasks or its PoCs). Rescheduling does not count.
  - Each row has **Nota** (writes a note in the account), **Abrir** (goes to the account) and **Silenciar** (hides it for another 7 days).
  - They also appear in the daily digest.
- **Ajustes**: the inactivity card is now "PoCs, RFPs y deals sin movimiento", with a new **Deals** row (3 to 30 days, or No avisar; 7 by default).

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.8.0-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Quit Notes Desk and drag **Notes Desk.app** into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Notes Desk.app"
   ```
