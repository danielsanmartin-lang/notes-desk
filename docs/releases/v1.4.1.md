Change in **Notes Desk**: dated items don't show on Home until their day.

## Changes

- **Home respects dates**: a follow-up or task with a date only shows on Home when its day arrives. Also:
  - **starred items (★) with a future date** wait for their date (before, they showed under "Starred" right away) and that day they show under "For today";
  - an **undated item whose open subtasks all have a date** no longer shows under "No date": each subtask shows up on its day. If any subtask has no date and is on you, the item stays under "No date".

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.4.1-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Drag the app inside the zip into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/<app name>.app"
   ```
