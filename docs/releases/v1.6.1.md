Finished PoCs now go to **PoCs concluidas**, and Estadísticas shows how long PoCs last in each region.

## Changes

- **Finished PoCs move to "PoCs concluidas"**: when you mark a PoC as finished, it leaves "PoCs en curso" (in the sidebar and on the board).
  - "PoCs concluidas" has a "Finalizadas" filter and a "Finalizadas · Pendientes de decisión" tile next to Ganadas and Perdidas, and its counter includes them.
  - Until the client decides, the PoC's tasks keep showing in Inicio, and its amount still counts as in progress in Estadísticas. The win rate still only counts won versus lost.
- **Average PoC duration per region**: each region card in Estadísticas (Iberia, LATAM, Sin país) shows the average duration and how many PoCs it is based on.
  - It counts PoCs that have ended (finished, won or lost), from the start date to the day the trial ended. If a PoC was finished and later won, the wait for the decision doesn't count.
- The PoC counts on the region cards are now in two rows, so they no longer overlap.

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.6.1-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Quit Notes Desk and drag **Notes Desk.app** into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Notes Desk.app"
   ```
