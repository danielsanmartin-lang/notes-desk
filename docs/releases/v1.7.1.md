Ajustes now has a **Guardar** button, you choose when PoCs and RFPs warn about inactivity, and the PoC and RFP task lists are edited row by row.

## Changes

- **Guardar button in Ajustes**: changes to follow-ups, the daily digest, quick capture, opening at login and inactivity alerts are no longer saved as you touch them.
  - A bar at the bottom says **Cambios sin guardar** while something is pending. **Guardar** saves it and confirms with "Ajustes guardados"; **Descartar** goes back to what was saved.
  - Appearance and theme still apply instantly, and the PoC and RFP task lists keep their own Guardar.
- **PoCs y RFPs sin movimiento**: a new card in Ajustes sets how many days without anything new (no task, follow-up, touch, reply or note) before a PoC or an RFP is flagged, separately for each: 3 to 30 days, or **No avisar**. Both start at 7 days.
  - RFPs in progress (in preparation or submitted) now get this warning too, in Inicio and in the daily digest.
  - In Inicio the group is called "PoCs sin movimiento", "RFPs sin movimiento" or "PoCs y RFPs sin movimiento", depending on what is in it.
- **Tareas de PoC / Tareas de RFP**: the suggested tasks in Ajustes are now a list of rows. Click one to edit it, remove it, or add new ones, then press Guardar.

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.7.1-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Quit Notes Desk and drag **Notes Desk.app** into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Notes Desk.app"
   ```
