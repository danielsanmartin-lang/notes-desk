Second version of **Notes Desk**, focused on projects.

## What's new

### Projects
- **Iberia and LATAM**: "Projects in progress" shows Iberia (Spain, Portugal and Andorra) first and LATAM below, with the total amount per currency for each region. Projects without a country go to "No country".
- **Amount** on each project: the HubSpot deal's or, without a deal, one entered by hand in € or US$. The currency follows the country (EUR in Iberia, USD in LATAM) until you change it.
- **Filters, sorting and list view**: search (name, company, partner), status, country and owner; sort by amount, start, creation, expected close, progress, last activity or name; cards or table view. Remembered between sessions.
- **Closed projects**: a separate section for won and lost ones, with success rate and amount won.
- **Country from a list** with search, flag and name, when creating the project and in its header. Codes that don't exist can no longer be saved (Mexico is MX).
- **Days open** on cards, list and header; for closed ones, how long it lasted.
- **Partner** of the project and new people lists: main contact, client admins and partner contact.
- **HubSpot importer** (`scripts/import-hubspot-pocs.js`): creates or updates the projects of your deals in the PoC stage with their open tasks, without duplicates.

### Stats
- **All · Iberia · LATAM** selector that filters everything.
- Cards per region, projects per country, days in PoC, expected closes per month and activity per region.

### Other
- In Today, Upcoming, Follow-ups and Accounts, project tasks carry a **PoC** label followed by the project name.
- An item's side panel opens and closes with a smooth transition.

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.1.0-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Drag the app inside the zip into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/<app name>.app"
   ```
