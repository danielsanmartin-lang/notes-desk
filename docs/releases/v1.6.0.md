New version of **Notes Desk**: RFPs get their own space with deadlines and documentation, PoCs can be marked as finished while the client decides, and Inicio warns you about PoCs that have gone quiet.

## What's new

- **RFPs**: a new "RFPs en curso" / "RFPs concluidas" section, next to PoCs. An RFP has the same checklist, contacts, notes, history, HubSpot deal and account as a PoC, plus:
  - Questions deadline, submission deadline (with a countdown on the card, the page and the sidebar), expected award date, tender portal and file number.
  - Stages: in preparation → submitted → awarded, lost or declined (we didn't bid). Closed RFPs show their win rate and awarded amount.
  - **Documentation**: drag files onto the RFP (or use the button). They are kept in a folder next to your data, open with their Mac app or show in Finder, and go to the Trash when removed. Files you drop into that folder from Finder show up too.
  - Inicio and the daily digest list **RFPs with a submission due** in the next 7 days, and overdue ones until you mark them submitted.
  - A "Tareas de RFP" template in Settings.
- **"Finalizada" PoCs**: for clients who try the product and take a long time to decide. Mark it from **Estado → Marcar como finalizada**.
  - It stays in "PoCs en curso", with its own filter and a flag in the sidebar.
  - Its tasks keep showing in Inicio, and it no longer appears in "PoCs sin movimiento".
  - The PoC page shows when it finished and has **Ganada** / **Perdida** buttons for when the client decides. The day counter stops on the day it finished.
  - Statistics and the week view count finished PoCs.
- **"PoCs sin movimiento" in Inicio**: active PoCs with 7 days or more without anything new in the PoC or its account, with Note, Open and Silence (for a week). They also go in the daily digest.
- **Snooze in one click**: rows due today or overdue show +1 day / Monday / +1 week on hover. A reminder moves with the task, and the notice can undo it.
- **New-task menu in rows**: type (Follow-up, Task, Note), company (search or create one on the spot), PoC or RFP, and date.
  - **Notes**: "Note" saves the text to the history of the PoC or, if there's none, of the company. Typing `#nota` also works, including in quick capture.
  - Clicking anywhere on "Otra" opens the calendar.
- Clicking a company on a task row opens its account page instead of HubSpot.

## Install or update

1. Download `Notes-Desk-1.6.0-mac-arm64.zip` (Mac with Apple Silicon) and unzip it.
2. Quit Notes Desk and drag **Notes Desk.app** into **Applications** (it replaces the previous one; your data is kept and updated the first time it opens).
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it → **Open**. If macOS says it is damaged, run:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Notes Desk.app"
   ```
